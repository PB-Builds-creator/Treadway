Set up Treadway on a new phone

Takes about two minutes. Do these in order on the phone you're setting up. You'll only ever do this once per phone.

  1. Open the app in SafariGo to cairn.surge.sh in Safari.
  2. Sign inEnter your email and password. (You'll only type these once — the app stays signed in after this.)
  3. Set your 4-digit codeWhen asked, pick your unlock code and enter it twice to confirm. From now on the app opens to this code — no password.
  4. Add it to your Home ScreenTap the Share button (the square with an up-arrow) → Add to Home Screen → Add. This is required for reminders to work.
  5. Open Treadway from the Home Screen iconNot from Safari — tap the new Treadway icon on your Home Screen. Enter your code.
  6. Turn on remindersTap Settings → Reminders → Allow. A "Reminders on" note pops up — that means it worked.
That's it. This phone is now set up, private to you, and will get your reminders. Nothing else to configure.

Same six steps work for any future phone or tablet. Reminders are turned on per-device, so each phone you want nudges on does step 6 once.
