Privacy in Cairn

A daily journal should be quiet, useful, and honest about where its data goes.

What Cairn stores

Your account email and profile name; tasks and schedules; daily completions; hydration and measured values; reflections and Cairn Close entries; partner-link status; reminder preferences; and the technical push-subscription details needed to deliver notifications.

Why it is used

This data powers your daily path, history, Weekly Trail, syncing, reminders, exports, and the partner features you explicitly enable. Cairn does not include advertising or behavioral analytics.

Partner sharing

When two accounts link, each person may see the other’s profile name and daily completion counts. A Proud nudge contains the sender’s name and the fixed words “Proud of you.” Tasks, task titles, hydration, measured values, and journal text are never shared with a partner.

Where data lives

Synced records are stored in the app’s Supabase project and protected by authenticated row-level access rules. A local cache also remains on signed-in devices so the interface can open quickly. Signing out or deleting the account clears that account’s Cairn cache from the device.

Services and retention

Supabase provides account, database, and server-function infrastructure; Surge serves the static web app; and the device’s browser push service carries requested notifications. Cairn keeps synced records while your account exists. Provider backups and operational logs may remain for limited periods under those providers’ own policies.

Security limits

Connections use HTTPS and account access rules separate members’ records. Reflections are not end-to-end encrypted. Reminder notifications may reveal a task title, or a partner’s name with a Proud nudge, on the device lock screen.

Your controls

Settings → Data & privacy lets you download your Cairn data, change your password, read this notice, or permanently delete your account and synced records. Partner links can be removed from Settings; unlinking revokes the relationship for both accounts.

Early-access status

This is the privacy overview for Cairn’s private early-access release, not a finalized public-sale privacy policy. Before a public commercial launch, Cairn will publish the operator identity, a support contact, effective terms, and a formal notice-change process. Early-access members can contact the person who invited them.
