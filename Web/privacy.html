Trailhead Privacy Policy

Trailhead is a private daily journal and habit tracker. This policy explains what the service handles, why, and the choices you have.

Effective July 22, 2026Last updated July 22, 2026

1. Who operates Trailhead

Trailhead is operated by Paxton Raithel in Colorado, United States. For support, privacy questions, or requests about your information, email paxtonraithel@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy applies to the Trailhead web app and progressive web app, including its account, journal, tracking, reminder, export, and partner features. It does not cover websites or services that publish their own privacy policies.

3. Information Trailhead handles

Trailhead handles information you provide or create, including:

  • Account and profile information, such as your email address, display name, accent choice, and account status.
  • Daily activity, such as tasks, schedules, completion history, streak and rest-day data, hydration, measured values, and goals.
  • Journal content, including reflections, wins, honest lines, and tomorrow intentions. This may contain sensitive personal information if you choose to write it.
  • Partner information, including link status, the linked person’s display name, daily completion counts, and Proud nudge status.
  • Reminder and technical information, such as reminder preferences, push-subscription identifiers, browser/device details, IP addresses, and security or service logs generated when Trailhead and its providers operate the service.

Trailhead also keeps an account-specific cache on signed-in devices so the app can open quickly and continue limited offline use.

4. How information is used

Trailhead uses this information to authenticate you; provide and sync your daily path, journal, history, Weekly Trail, exports, partner features, and requested reminders; maintain account security; prevent abuse; troubleshoot the service; respond to support and privacy requests; and meet legal obligations. Trailhead does not use third-party behavioral analytics or build advertising profiles.

5. Partner sharing

Partner sharing starts only after two accounts explicitly link. A linked partner can receive your display name and daily completion counts. A Proud nudge contains the sender’s display name and the fixed words “Proud of you.” Trailhead does not share task titles, task details, hydration, measured values, or journal text with a partner. Either person can unlink at any time, which revokes the relationship for both accounts.

6. Service providers and other disclosures

Trailhead uses a limited set of providers to run the service:

  • Supabase provides authentication, database storage, and server functions.
  • Surge hosts and delivers the static web app and may process short-lived operational request logs for reliability, security, and abuse prevention.
  • Browser and device push services deliver notifications you choose to enable.

These providers process information for Trailhead under their own service terms and privacy commitments. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, protect a person’s safety, investigate abuse, or defend Trailhead’s rights. If Trailhead is transferred to another operator, information may transfer with the service subject to this policy and any required notice.

Trailhead does not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use it for targeted advertising.

7. Retention and deletion

Trailhead generally keeps synced information while your account remains open. You can permanently delete your account from Settings → Data & privacy; this deletes the account and its synced profile, tasks, history, hydration, measurements, reflections, partner link, reminders, and related app records. Signing out or deleting the account clears that account’s Trailhead cache from that device. Limited provider backups, fraud-prevention records, and operational or security logs may remain for a reasonable period under provider retention practices or legal requirements.

8. Security and notification privacy

Trailhead uses HTTPS, authenticated access, and database row-level access rules intended to separate members’ records and limit partner access. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Journal content is not end-to-end encrypted, which means authorized service infrastructure can technically process it. Notifications can reveal a task title or a partner’s name and Proud nudge on the device lock screen; you can disable reminders in Trailhead or adjust notification previews in device settings.

9. Your choices and privacy requests

Settings → Data & privacy lets you download a JSON copy of your Trailhead data, change your password, review this policy, or delete your account. You can edit profile and tracker information in the app, disable reminders, and unlink a partner.

You may also email paxtonraithel@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion, or a portable copy of your information. Trailhead may need to verify your identity before completing a request and will respond within the period required by applicable law. If a request is denied, reply with “Privacy appeal” in the subject line and explain why you believe the decision should be reconsidered. Because Trailhead does not sell personal information or use targeted advertising, there is no sale or targeted-advertising processing to opt out of, including through Global Privacy Control.

10. Children

Trailhead is not directed to children under 13, and the operator does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact the operator so the information can be deleted.

11. Where information is processed

Trailhead and its providers may process information in the United States and other locations where they operate. Those locations may have data-protection rules different from the place where you live.

12. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, Trailhead will post the revised policy here and update the date above. For material changes, Trailhead will provide advance notice by email or a prominent in-app notice when practical and legally required. The notice will explain when the revised policy takes effect. Continued use after that effective date means the revised policy applies to later use; where consent is legally required, Trailhead will request it.
